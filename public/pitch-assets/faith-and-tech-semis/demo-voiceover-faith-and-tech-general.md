## Part 1: understand the person and the need

**Clip:** `Screen Recording 2026-11-18 at 18.02.36.mov` (2:09.5).  
**On screen:** Miracle's interview, cause review, fee advice, identity step and publication.

Miracle needs three hundred thousand naira for her University of Lagos fees. Kairos helps her explain the need clearly, without expecting her to know how to write a fundraising appeal.

The AI follows what she says. It asks about her studies, circumstances, deadline and payment reference, and requests evidence. Miracle reviews the resulting budget and attaches her payment advice before publishing. She can add supporting documents later too.

We want a donor to understand both the person and the bill. We also need to protect the person asking. Draft attachments are private today, but published attachments are public. Private review of sensitive evidence is an important next step.

For an expired payment reference, our planned flow asks for a replacement when payment is due. An uncertain check or a mismatch goes to a human reviewer, who records a decision before payment continues. That review workflow is still to be built.

## Part 2: give from the shared cause

**Clip:** `Screen Recording 2026-11-18 at 18.12.46.mov` (0:37).  
**On screen:** An anonymous donor opens the cause in a separate browser and contributes.

The cause link can be shared on WhatsApp. A donor can read the story and bill, then give without opening an account. That gift is anonymous, as the page explains.

Here, someone gives one hundred and fifty thousand naira. The final ledger records one hundred and forty-seven thousand for the fees and three thousand in upkeep.

## Part 3: see the cause become fully funded

**Clip:** `Screen Recording 2026-11-18 at 18.27.54.mov` (0:37).  
**On screen:** Abigail gives and both account views update.

Abigail supplies the remaining one hundred and fifty-three thousand naira, plus two thousand in upkeep. The fees are now fully funded.

Our tentative upkeep model is two percent, capped at five thousand per cause. Grants and sponsorship would help support the service, but are not secured. Each donor can see the amount credited towards the actual need.

## Part 4: account for what each gift paid for

**Clip:** `Screen Recording 2026-11-18 at 18.29.44.mov` (1:04.7).  
**On screen:** Provider payment, application receipt, public ledger, Abigail's notification and email.

Miracle requests payment to the school through the named route. She cannot withdraw the cause funds personally. Abigail then receives a record showing her own share of the payment, while everyone can follow the public ledger.

The AI, uploads, ledger and alerts work. Identity checks and external settlement are simulated. Live operation needs licensed partners and reconciliation with independently confirmed payments. The invoice here is our application record.

Our next step is a small pilot with students, donors and an institution, recording what they need and what we change. We want giving to come with a clear account of what happened next.
