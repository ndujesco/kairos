## Slide 4: Technical approach and donor accounting

Abigail or Ugochukwu · 1:20 to 2:00

Technically, Next.js handles the app, Anthropic powers the interview, MongoDB stores the records, and Cloudinary stores uploads. The server checks the available balance and budget before recording a payout.

A donor doesn't just get a thank you, they get a receipt which tells them exactly what their money was used for. This table shows each donor's share of the school-fee payment. Upkeep is disclosed before giving.

## Short explanation

Kairos makes crowdfunding transparent. A published need and itemised budget name the provider to be paid; each donor can follow their own contribution through the cause records.

The live AI interview, uploads, internal accounting and notifications work. Identity lookup and settlement in this demonstration are simulated; licensed partners and private evidence review are still needed.

Our next step is a small school-fee pilot with students, donors and an institution, using actual findings to improve the flow.
