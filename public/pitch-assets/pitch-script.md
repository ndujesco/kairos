## Short explanation

Kairos is a transparent giving platform. Think of a hospital appeal on WhatsApp: you want to help, but an account number and a transfer confirmation don't tell you whether the bill was paid.

On Kairos, the organiser publishes an itemised budget naming the hospital, school or supplier. The intended live flow holds donations with a licensed partner and pays those providers directly. Every donor receives a receipt showing their own share of each payment.

The prototype already demonstrates the accounting, budget checks, donor attribution and notifications. Bank transfers, Remita settlement and NIN lookup are simulated. Provider checks and licensed custody still need to be connected, and no real donations have moved.

We would start with school fees and a few pilot communities, then hospital bills. The aim is a clearer account for donors and organisers, so more genuine needs can earn support.

## Optional 90-second demo

### Open the cause (15 seconds)

This is a seeded demonstration. Here is the need, its budget and the provider named against each expense. The money figures are demo accounting.

On screen: Have a rehearsed cause and two separate signed-in browser sessions ready. Open the cause page; show the named provider and budget.

### Make a demo contribution (20 seconds)

Before confirming, the donor sees the upkeep in naira and can cover it. The recorded contribution increases this cause's available balance.

On screen: Show the fee breakdown and cover option. Use the simulated transfer confirmation. Do not call it a live bank payment.

### Request the provider payment (20 seconds)

The organiser can request a payment against this budget item. There is no cash-withdrawal path. The code checks the available balance and the amount left on this line.

On screen: Switch to the organiser's separate session. Show the named budget item, then record the rehearsed demo payout. Say that settlement and provider verification are simulated or unbuilt.

### Show the donor's receipt (20 seconds)

Now the donor sees their own share, the provider and the invoice. This is the connection between the contribution and the bill.

On screen: Open the donor's in-app notification and read the actual displayed amount. Show the same payout in the public history. Do not substitute the deck's illustrative ₦4,900 if this run gives another number.

### State the next step (15 seconds)

The accounting and donor updates are running. Bank transfer, Remita settlement and NIN lookup are simulated. Live custody and provider checks are the next integration work.

On screen: Stop the demo. If a page stalls, finish the sentence and use the existing recording. Treat the recording as an older prototype demonstration, not evidence of live verification or settlement.
