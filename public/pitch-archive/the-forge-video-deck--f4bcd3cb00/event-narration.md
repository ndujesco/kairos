# Kairos - live narration over the demo video

For speaking on stage while `video/kairos-demo-master.mp4` plays behind you.

The video runs 2:10. This is about 2:05 of speech, so it is close to continuous.
There is one deliberate pause, near the end, and it is marked. Everything else
you just read straight through at a normal talking pace, around 150 words a
minute.

The small grey cues are only there so you know what the screen is doing while
you talk. Ignore them and the words still run as one paragraph.

---

## Before you press play

> Let me show you the product. This is not a mockup, and it is not a slideshow
> of screenshots. It is the real thing, running, recorded in one pass.

*(press play)*

---

## The narration

*0:00 · the feed, cursor moving over the verified tick, the raised figure, the vouches*

> So this is Kairos, running. Every organizer here has verified their identity,
> and every number on these cards is real: a hundred and fifty five thousand
> raised, three donors, and vouches from people staking their own name on it.

*0:15 · Chidinma's cause opens, then Copy link flips to "Link copied"*

> Open any cause and you get the whole story, what has been raised, and what is
> still sitting in escrow. Then you donate, or you copy the link, and whoever
> you send it to sees this exact page. No app, no signup.

*0:30 · the verification panel, then the budget, resting on the LUTH line*

> This is what no other platform shows you. Every piece of evidence, checked.
> And the budget, itemized: corrective surgery, eight hundred and fifty thousand
> naira, going straight to LUTH, a verified vendor with its account on record.
> Never to the organizer.

*0:45 · the invoice, photos from the ward, then the public ledger scrolling*

> Money already spent leaves a trail. That is the real invoice, number 0042,
> four hundred thousand naira. Photos from the ward, posted as the work
> happened. And a public ledger, every naira in and every naira out, open to
> anyone.

*1:00 · the answer is sent, "typing…", then the AI pushes back*

> Creating a cause is not a form, it is an interview, and that is a live model
> thinking. Watch it push back: my brother needs surgery is not enough for it,
> it wants the exact date and the hospital's full name.

*1:15 · the filled form, then five fraud checks tick green and the cause publishes*

> Everything it collected comes out as a structured form: story, evidence,
> budget rows. Then it screens the whole thing, timeline consistency, vendor
> naming, budget specificity. Five checks, and nothing publishes until all five
> pass. That one just did.

*1:30 · David's escrow wallet, then the vendor payout form*

> And here is the heart of it, the organizer's wallet. A hundred and fifty five
> thousand naira, held in escrow. Now look for a withdraw button. There is none,
> anywhere in the product. He can only pay a vendor on his published budget.

*1:45 · the donor's receipt card, the long scene*

> And the moment that payment leaves escrow, every donor's phone buzzes. Four
> thousand five hundred and sixty naira of your seven thousand, paid to LUTH,
> invoice 0042, sixty five percent of your gift.

*(stop here. count three seconds. look at the screen, not the room)*

> Nobody wrote that message. The platform worked it out for every donor, the
> instant the money moved.

*the video ends*

> That is not a thank you note. That is a receipt. Crowdfunding platforms move
> money. Kairos moves proof.

Then go to your next slide, or take questions.

## Notes for the room

- **The video is the clock, not you.** If you fall behind, drop the rest of the
  paragraph and pick up the next one. Nobody knows what you planned to say.
- **The four numbers that must come out cleanly** are ₦850,000 to LUTH,
  ₦155,000 in escrow, ₦4,560 of ₦7,000, and 65%. Everything else can be
  paraphrased. Those four are the argument.
- **"No withdraw button" is the line people repeat afterwards.** Land it, slow
  down on it, and keep the three second pause on the receipt even if you are
  running long. If you need to buy time, lose "Photos from the ward, posted as
  the work happened" or "a verified vendor with its account on record" instead.
  They are the only two lines carrying nothing essential.
- The scenes cut every fifteen seconds whether or not you have finished
  speaking, so finish the sentence and let the next paragraph start a beat late.
  It never drifts more than a second or two.
- If someone asks afterwards: yes, the AI is a live model, and it asks different
  follow-up questions on every take. It is not a recording of a script.
- If the video will not play, `video/scenes/` has the same footage split into
  eight clips, and the still screenshots are on slides 5 to 12 of the deck.
