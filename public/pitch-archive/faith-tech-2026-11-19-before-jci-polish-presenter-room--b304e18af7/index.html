Transparent crowdfunding

Good evening everyone. We are Team Kairos. Kairos is a transparent crowdfunding platform created to solve the problem of trust when someone asks for help and several people donate their hard earned money. Every naira you give can be followed until it reaches the need it was given for.

02 / 060:25 to 0:55Abigail

The trust gap

Think of a student who needs help with school fees. Someone shares her appeal on WhatsApp. You want to help, but you still have questions. Is the bill real? How much is actually owed? And after you give, how do you know the fees were paid? That hesitation is the problem we are solving.

DELIVERY NOTEA familiar scenario, not a claim that this example is a verified real case.

03 / 060:55 to 1:30Abigail

How Kairos works

How do we do this? An AI interviewer asks about the need, the institution, the amount and supporting documents. That becomes a cause with an itemised budget naming who should be paid. People contribute to that bill. The organiser can request a provider payment, but there is no personal withdrawal. We make that possible by controlling the flow of money.

04 / 061:30 to 2:05Abigail

Every giver sees their own part

A donor doesn't just get a thank you, they get a receipt which tells them exactly what their money was used for. In our school-fee example, each donor sees their share of the three hundred thousand naira payment. Before giving, they see the upkeep. Our tentative fallback is two percent, capped at five thousand naira per cause, while we seek grants and sponsorship.

18 of 18 questions
What does Kairos do?Rehearse first001 · The giving flow

It connects a published need and budget to provider payments and each donor's share. The prototype implements the accounting and updates; live custody and settlement still require integration.

What stops the organizer withdrawing donations?Rehearse first002 · The giving flow

The prototype has no personal cash-withdrawal path and checks payment permissions, balance and budget. Live operation needs a licensed custody/payment arrangement that enforces the same restrictions.

Does the receipt independently prove the school received money?Rehearse first003 · The giving flow

The current receipt records a simulated application payout. A live settled receipt must be tied to confirmed provider settlement; a locally generated invoice alone is insufficient.

Can someone give without an account?004 · The giving flow

Yes. Guest gifts are anonymous and appear in the public cause records. Signed-in donors can receive personal in-app updates; email is optional.

What if a Remita reference expires?Rehearse first005 · Review and privacy

The agreed proposed flow pauses payout, asks the organizer through the AI for a replacement, verifies the same student, institution and bill, then continues. This is not yet implemented.

When does a human step in?Rehearse first006 · Review and privacy

The proposed workflow escalates a mismatch or an unresolved check. The reviewer checks evidence, records the decision and permits an appropriate next step. A dedicated reviewer workflow is not built.

Does AI authenticate documents or identify fraud?Rehearse first007 · Review and privacy

It helps structure the need and can discuss consistency. Uploads are accepted supporting material. Independent document authentication, image-reuse checks and live registry checks are not operational.

Who can view supporting files today?Rehearse first008 · Review and privacy

Unpublished files are owner-only. Files attached to published causes are publicly viewable through the app. Sensitive evidence needs a separate access/redaction design before live use.

Do you store a donor's or organizer's NIN?009 · Review and privacy

The current identity endpoint stores the simulated verification result and method, not the NIN number. This does not establish that live identity verification or a complete launch privacy program is in place.

How does the fee cap work?Rehearse first010 · Fees and records

The current rule takes 2% of each gross gift until total upkeep reaches ₦5,000 for that cause. It is a per-cause cap. The charge and net cause credit are shown before confirmation.

How are donor shares calculated?Rehearse first011 · Fees and records

The allocation uses the donor's net contribution after upkeep. In the full school-fee example, Abigail's ₦155,000 gross payment credits ₦153,000 and that net amount is attributed to the school's recorded payout.

Have you reconciled real bank payments?Rehearse first012 · Fees and records

No. Internal balances and payment allocations run in the prototype. External reconciliation needs unique references and a match to confirmed provider settlement, including pending, failed and duplicate events.

Is NGO funding already secured?013 · Fees and records

No. Donations and NGO grants are the intended primary support, with tentative capped upkeep as a fallback. The prototype currently applies its upkeep rule; no grant-triggered switch or proven unit economics is claimed.

Are refunds implemented?014 · Fees and records

A complete production refund and dispute process is still to be designed and integrated. The prototype does not prove that operation.

What user research supports this presentation?Rehearse first015 · Evidence and launch

The supplied materials contain the team's school-fee observation, but no documented interview findings were provided for this draft. The team should add actual dated findings and their design implications before claiming user validation.

What would the first pilot measure?016 · Evidence and launch

Start with school-fee bills settled, donor understanding of receipts, unresolved payment issues and repeat giving. These are proposed measures, not completed pilot results.

What remains before live donations?Rehearse first017 · Evidence and launch

Licensed custody and live payments, independent verification, appropriate private-evidence controls, external reconciliation and an exception/refund process. No signed custody partnership is claimed.

How long is this pitch allowed to be?018 · Evidence and launch

The confirmed format is one minute for team introduction, seven minutes for presentation and demo, five minutes for Q&A and two minutes for scoring/transition. The user has allocated three minutes to the pitch; demo timing is separate.
