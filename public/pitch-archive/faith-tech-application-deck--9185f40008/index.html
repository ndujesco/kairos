Slide 5 · KAIROS

Original speaker notes

Bank transfer, Remita settlement and NIN lookup are simulated. CAC and bank account-name verification are unbuilt. General file uploads and automated document verification should not be claimed as implemented. The Claude integration has a scripted fallback when unavailable. This deck makes no claim of live donors or a completed pilot.

Slide 6 · KAIROS

Original speaker notes

The cost-recovery model is proposed, not proven unit economics. The prototype shows the upkeep amount before confirmation and allows the donor to cover it. The 2% rate and ₦5,000 cap are tentative. Grants, credits and sponsorship are intended sources for shared operating costs, not confirmed awards or partnerships.

Slide 7 · KAIROS

Original speaker notes

These are launch plans. A pilot handling real donations has not been run. School fees would use institutional payment references such as Remita; hospital payouts require provider checks and a licensed partner.

Slide 8 · KAIROS

Original speaker notes
